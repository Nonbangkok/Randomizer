How to Install and Play the Elden Ring Randomizer Mod Safely

*Elden Ring* is a massive game, and once you've conquered it multiple times, you might be looking for a new challenge. The **Elden Ring randomizer mod** shuffles items, enemies, and even bosses to provide a fresh experience. However, since the game has strict anti-cheat measures, installing it **safely** is the most important step. Here is your complete guide.

Safety First: Avoiding the Ban

Elden Ring uses Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC). If you try to play online with mods, you *will* be banned. To play safely:

  • Mod Engine 2: Use Mod Engine 2 to launch the game. It automatically disables EAC and prevents the game from connecting to FromSoftware's servers.
  • Offline Mode: Always ensure your Steam is in "Offline" mode or use the Mod Engine launcher which handles this for you.
  • Separate Saves: Keep your modded saves separate from your vanilla saves. The randomizer often creates a separate file automatically, but it's good practice to back up your original saves manually.

Installation Steps

  1. Download Mod Engine 2: This is the framework you’ll use to load the mod. Extract it to its own folder.
  2. Download the Randomizer: Get the "Elden Ring Item and Enemy Randomizer" from Nexus Mods.
  3. Configure the Randomizer: Open the randomizer's .exe and point it to your Elden Ring installation. Choose your settings—like "Randomize Bosses" or "Scavenger Mode."
  4. Generate Seeds: Click "Randomize" to generate the modded files. These will usually go into a `mod` folder inside your Mod Engine 2 directory.
  5. Launch: Run the `launchmod_eldenring.bat` file in your Mod Engine 2 folder. You’ll know it’s working if you see the "Easy Anti-Cheat is disabled" message.

What to Expect in Your Run

In a randomized run, Malenia might be waiting for you in the first cave of the game, while the Elden Beast is a random soldier in Limgrave. You’ll need to explore every corner of the map to find the key items needed to progress. It’s a true test of your knowledge of the Lands Between.

Conclusion

Installing the **elden ring randomizer mod safely** allows you to experience the game in a whole new way without risking your account. It turns a familiar journey into a dangerous, unpredictable, and highly rewarding adventure. Good luck, Tarnished!
