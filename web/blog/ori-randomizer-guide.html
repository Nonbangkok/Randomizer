Ori and the Will of the Wisps Randomizer: How to Install and Play

*Ori and the Will of the Wisps* is one of the most visually stunning and mechanically tight Metroidvanias ever made. But once you've 100% completed it, what's next? Enter the **Ori and the Will of the Wisps randomizer**, a mod that shuffles abilities, shards, and life/spirit cells to give the game infinite replayability. Here is your guide on how to get started.

What is Randomized?

The mod shuffles almost all collectible items in the world. You might find "Double Jump" in a hidden corner of Luma Pools, or "Bash" in the Kwolok’s Hollow. This forces you to explore the game in a completely non-linear way, often requiring creative use of your current abilities to reach new areas.

Installation Steps

  1. The Base Game: You'll need a legitimate copy of the game on PC (Steam or Windows Store).
  2. Download the Randomizer: Head over to the official community GitHub or Discord to find the latest "WotW Randomizer" launcher.
  3. Run the Setup: The launcher will automatically detect your game installation and patch the necessary files. Don't worry, it usually doesn't affect your vanilla save files.
  4. Generate a Seed: Choose your settings—like "Balanced" or "Hardcore"—and generate a unique seed.
  5. Launch: Use the randomizer launcher to start the game. You'll know it worked if the main menu has a custom randomizer logo!

Tips for Your First Run

  • Check Your Map: The randomizer includes a custom map that shows which items are "reachable" with your current set of abilities. This is a lifesaver for beginners.
  • Master Your Shards: Since you might not have key abilities early on, combat shards like "Spirit Smash" or "Sentry" become much more important.
  • Don't Panic: If you feel stuck, the "Logic" of the randomizer ensures that there is *always* a way to progress. You might just need to find a hidden secret or master a difficult jump.

Conclusion

The **Ori and the Will of the Wisps randomizer** breathes new life into Niwen. It turns a beautiful story into a challenging, unpredictable puzzle that will test your platforming skills and game knowledge like never before.
