Roblox Fans: The Best Games Like Randomizer You Should Try

The original *Randomizer* on Roblox created a new sub-genre of combat games where skill is secondary to adaptability. Every time you spawn, you get a new set of weapons and items, forcing you to constantly change your playstyle. If you love that chaos, here are the **best games like Randomizer** that you should try today.

1. Item Asylum

Arguably the most popular "Randomizer-like" game on the platform. It features hundreds of items—from anime references to classic Roblox memes. The combat is fast, hilarious, and completely unpredictable. If you like the sheer variety of *Randomizer*, this is your next stop.

2. Allusions

Similar to Item Asylum but with a slightly darker, more gothic aesthetic. It features a massive array of items based on various mythologies, creepypastas, and other games. The focus is on unique "movesets" that come with each item.

3. Typical Colors 2 (Randomizer Mode)

While TC2 is primarily a class-based shooter, its Randomizer mode is legendary. It shuffles classes and weapons, leading to hilarious combinations like a Heavy with a Scout's speed or a Spy with a Rocket Launcher. It’s a great way to experience a polished shooter with a chaotic twist.

4. Lab Experiment

Instead of just weapons, this game randomizes the entire environment. Every round, a new "experiment" or event happens to the players on a small plate. It’s less about combat and more about surviving the randomness of the world itself.

Conclusion

The success of the "Randomizer" genre on Roblox proves that players crave variety and surprise. These **games like Randomizer** offer the same core thrill: the excitement of spawning with a new kit and figuring out how to win with it. Dive in and embrace the chaos!
