Why Use a Team Randomizer?

Hand-picking teams often leads to "friendship clusters" or unbalanced skill levels. A randomizer tool ensures that:

  • Fairness: Every participant has an equal chance of being in any group.
  • Diversity: People are forced to interact with others they might not usually choose.
  • Speed: You can split 100 people into 10 teams in a single click.
