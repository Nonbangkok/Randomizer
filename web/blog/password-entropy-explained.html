Understanding Password Entropy: How Secure is Your Code?

When you use a password generator, you often see a "bits" or "entropy" score. But what does it actually mean? In the world of cybersecurity, entropy is the measure of randomness or unpredictability in a password. The higher the entropy, the harder it is for a computer to guess your password.

The Math of Guessing

Entropy is usually measured in "bits." A password with 1 bit of entropy has 2 possible combinations (0 or 1). A password with 128 bits of entropy has 2128 combinations—a number so astronomical that even the world's most powerful supercomputers couldn't crack it in billions of years.

Length vs. Complexity

A common misconception is that adding "special characters" like @#$ is the best way to make a password strong. While it helps, length is actually a much more powerful driver of entropy. A long, simple phrase is often much more secure than a short, complex string that is hard to remember.

Conclusion
