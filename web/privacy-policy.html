Privacy Policy

At Randomizer, your privacy is our top priority. Our tools are designed to run entirely in your browser.

1. Local Processing

All randomization and generation (Names, Passwords, etc.) happen locally on your device using WebAssembly. We do not send your inputs or the generated results to any servers.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Our tools do not require an account or any form of identification.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (like Plausible or Umami) to understand general traffic patterns. These services do not use cookies and do not track individual users.

4. Third-Party Ads

We may display advertisements through services like Google AdSense. These services may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
