Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

At Randomizer, accessible from randomizer.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Randomizer and how we use it.

No Personal Data Collection

Randomizer is designed to be a "client-side only" utility. This means all generation logic (names, passwords, etc.) happens entirely within your web browser using WebAssembly (WASM) and JavaScript. We do not transmit, store, or see the data you generate.

Log Files

Randomizer follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to www.website.com and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks uses technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Randomizer, which are sent directly to users' browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that Randomizer has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.
